Questions to ask before work begins

You don’t need to diagnose the equipment yourself. Instead, prepare a straightforward account of the issue and ask the contractor to explain the work they recommend. For an installation, describe the property and your reason for considering a new system. For a repair, explain what changed and what you have already observed.

Before an appointment, consider this checklist:

  • Identify whether the request is for heating, air conditioning, or another HVAC concern.
  • Note any sounds, operating changes, or rooms affected by the issue.
  • Make sure the contractor knows how to reach the equipment inside the property.
  • Ask what the next step will be after the contractor reviews the system.

Avoid choosing a contractor based only on an assumption about the repair. Don’t open or dismantle equipment to try to confirm the cause. A clear description is more useful than a guess, and the contractor can assess the system directly.
