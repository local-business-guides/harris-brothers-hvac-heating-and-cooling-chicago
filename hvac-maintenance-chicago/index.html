What to ask and prepare before service

The exact work depends on your equipment and the reason for the visit. Before the appointment, gather any details you can about the system and describe what you’ve observed. You can ask what the contractor plans to check and whether they found anything that needs follow-up.

A short list can help you keep the conversation focused:

  • Note when the issue began and whether it happens every time the system runs.
  • Write down any changes in comfort, sounds, or system operation.
  • Share the system’s location and any access details for your home.
  • Ask what maintenance tasks are appropriate for you to do between professional visits.

Avoid guessing at a repair or taking equipment apart before the appointment. If the system has stopped working, tell the contractor rather than treating it as a routine maintenance request.
