What to expect from cooling service

Air conditioning work may include troubleshooting a system that isn’t cooling properly, repairing an identified issue, or installing equipment. The contractor first needs to understand what the system is doing and inspect the relevant equipment. The right next step depends on what’s found, so avoid deciding in advance that a particular part or full replacement is needed.

A useful checklist before calling:

  • Note when the cooling issue started and whether it comes and goes.
  • Record the thermostat setting and any unusual sounds or changes.
  • Find the equipment details if they’re easy to access.
  • Keep the area around the system clear for inspection.

Don’t keep switching the system on and off if it repeatedly stops working. Avoid DIY electrical or equipment repairs, and don’t cover or block parts of the system. If there’s an immediate safety concern, move away from the equipment and contact the appropriate emergency service. For a cooling problem that needs diagnosis, call an HVAC professional.
