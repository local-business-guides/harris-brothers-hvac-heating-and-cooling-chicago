What the installation process can cover

A furnace installation typically involves reviewing the existing setup, discussing a suitable replacement, and completing the agreed installation work. The exact scope depends on the home and the equipment being considered, so confirm the details with your contractor rather than assuming every project is the same.

Before the appointment, you can prepare a short checklist:

  • Make a note of heating problems, including when they occur.
  • Gather any available information about the current furnace.
  • List questions about the equipment, work area, and project timing.
  • Clear access to the furnace and the route workers may need to use.
  • Tell the contractor about household access needs or other job-site concerns.

Avoid choosing a system based only on a quick comparison of product names. Ask how the recommendation relates to your home and what work the installation includes. If you're unsure whether the system needs replacement or another kind of service, describe the symptoms and ask for an assessment.
